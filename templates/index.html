📖 Game Rules & Instructions

🃏 Animal Matching Card Game

  • Designed for 3 players with 12 shuffled cards.
  • The cards contain 3 animals: Lion 🦁 (4 cards), Tiger 🐯 (4 cards), and Elephant 🐘 (4 cards).
  • At the start, cards are shuffled and each player receives 4 cards.
  • Exchange Rule: Each round, every player selects 1 unwanted card from their hand to pass clockwise to the next player.
  • Objective: Be the first player to collect all 4 cards of the same animal (e.g. 4 Lions, 4 Tigers, or 4 Elephants) to win!
  • If playing solo or with 2 players, smart AI bots fill the empty seat(s) automatically.

🕵️ The Imposter

Citizens get the true secret word (e.g. "Coffee"). The Imposter receives a slightly different word (e.g. "Tea"). Players give one-word clues, discuss who gave a weird clue, and vote out the suspect!

🎬 Guess the Movie Name

Look at the emoji combination (e.g. 🦁👑 for The Lion King or 🚢❄️💔 for Titanic). Reveal hints for director, actors, or release year if stuck!

🎵 Guess the Song Name

Hit the Play button to listen to synthesized melody notes generated directly through Web Audio. Look at the emoji hints and lyrics to name the song!
